Answer to your question: PocketStash already uses Inter — its body font is Inter and its money class is just Inter with tabular-nums (no separate mono font). So "same as PocketStash" and "Inter" are the same choice — shown first above.

My recommendation: Inter tabular-nums too. One font for everything, numbers still align perfectly because of tabular figures, and both apps look like siblings. Mono fonts (Plex/Geist/…) only add value if you want money to look visibly "machine-typed" — a deliberate style, not a necessity.
