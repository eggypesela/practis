My recommendation: A Same look as PocketStash, one font to load, numbers still line up perfectly (tabular figures do that — not the font). Your two apps would feel like one product family, and you avoid a second font download.

Pick B only if you specifically like money looking "machine-typed" — doc numbers and dates in a typewriter face, which some people find easier to scan in long ledgers.
